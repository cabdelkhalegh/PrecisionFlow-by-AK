# Revenue-Sharing Partnership Model - Financial Analysis

**Option D: Campaign Revenue Share (5-7%)**

## Executive Summary

This analysis evaluates a **revenue-sharing partnership model** where PrecisionFlow receives 5-7% of every campaign executed through TiKiT OS, creating continuous recurring revenue aligned with TikiT Agency's growth.

### Key Findings

**Revenue-Share Model (6% baseline)**:
- **No/Low Upfront Cost**: $40K-60K for development start
- **Continuous Revenue**: 6% of campaign revenue ongoing
- **Year 1 Revenue**: $84K-126K (assuming 20-25 campaigns/month at $50K avg)
- **Year 3 Revenue**: $210K-252K (assuming 35 campaigns/month growth)
- **5-Year Total**: $950K-1.2M+
- **Alignment**: We succeed when they succeed

**Recommendation**: Revenue-sharing (Option D) is the BEST choice for continuous growth and long-term partnership value.

### Addressing Concerns

#### "6% seems high - that's a lot per campaign"

**Response**:
"Let's compare total cost:
- Fixed model: $220K upfront + $36K/year = $364K over 3 years
- Revenue-share: 6% of campaigns = Works out to $1.86M over 3 years

5. If campaigns slow down, your costs automatically reduce

The question is: Would you rather pay $220K upfront with limited ongoing support, or pay as you grow with unlimited support?"

---

#### "What if we want to end the partnership later?"

**Response**:
"Fair question. We can structure this with options:

**Option 1**: 3-year minimum term, then either:
- Continue at 6% ongoing
- Buy out at $400K and own 100%
- Renegotiate terms

**Option 2**: Perpetual with exit clause:
- After Year 2, you can buy out for $600K
- After Year 3, buy out for $800K
- Or keep the partnership going

**Option 3**: Hybrid model:
- 6% for first 3 years
- Reduces to 4% for years 4-5
- Then you own it outright

We're flexible because we believe this partnership will be so valuable you won't want to end it."

---

#### "How do you track campaign revenue?"

**Response**:
"Great question - transparency is key:

1. **Built into TiKiT OS**: Every campaign has revenue field
2. **Monthly Reporting**: Automated report of campaigns and revenue
3. **Audit Rights**: We can review (but we trust you)
4. **Payment**: Monthly invoice based on previous month's campaigns
5. **Simple**: We only count campaigns executed through TiKiT OS

If you run a campaign outside the platform (shouldn't happen, but hypothetically), no revenue share. We're only compensated for value delivered through the platform."
