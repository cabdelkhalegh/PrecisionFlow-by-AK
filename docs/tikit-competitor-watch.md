# TiKiT OS - Competitor Intelligence Report (Deep Dive)

## 🚨 Tier 1: The Incumbents (Primary Targets)

### 1. GRIN (The "Category King")
- **Target:** E-commerce Brands (DTC).
- **Pricing:** Hidden. Typically starts at **$24k/year** (annual contract required).
- **Core Pitch:** "Creator Management for Shopify."
- **Critical Weakness:**
    - **No Monthly Plan:** Locks customers into huge contracts.
    - **Shopify Only:** Useless for service agencies or B2B.
    - **No Real Workflow:** It's just a database + email tool.
- **TiKiT Attack Angle:** "Stop paying for features you don't use. Get monthly flexibility and real workflow automation."

### 2. Influencer Hero (The "AI Pretender")
- **Target:** Small to Mid-sized Agencies.
- **Pricing:** Starts at **$649/mo**.
- **Core Pitch:** "All-in-One AI."
- **Critical Weakness:**
    - **Fake AI:** Their "AI" is just search filters (not generative).
    - **Clunky CRM:** Users report it's hard to track conversation status.
- **TiKiT Attack Angle:** "We use Generative AI to *negotiate* deals, not just find emails. Our CRM is built by engineers, for engineers."

### 3. Upfluence (The "Enterprise Dinosaur")
- **Target:** Large Corporations.
- **Pricing:** **$1,000+/mo**.
- **Core Pitch:** "Largest Database."
- **Critical Weakness:**
    - **Slow:** The UI is dated and laggy.
    - **Sales-Led:** You have to talk to a salesperson to buy.
- **TiKiT Attack Angle:** "Self-Serve. Fast. Modern. No sales calls."

## 🧠 Strategic Vulnerabilities to Exploit

1.  **The "Database Myth":**
    -   Competitors sell "Access to 100M Influencers".
    -   **Reality:** Most of that data is public (Instagram API).
    -   **TiKiT Move:** Give the database for free. Charge for the **Workflow**.

2.  **The "Agency Tax":**
    -   Competitors charge per "seat" or "client".
    -   **TiKiT Move:** Unlimited seats. Unlimited clients. Usage-based pricing on *active campaigns*.

3.  **The "AI Hype":**
    -   Competitors slap "AI" on basic search filters.
    -   **TiKiT Move:** Show real **Generative AI** (writing briefs, predicting ROI).
