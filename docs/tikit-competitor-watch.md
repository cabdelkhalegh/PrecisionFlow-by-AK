## Detailed Notes

### CreatorIQ (Enterprise Benchmark)
- Source: [CreatorIQ site](https://www.creatoriq.com/), [PartnrUp pricing review](https://partnrup.ai/creatoriq-pricing/)
- Claims: "AI-powered intelligence, global governance, enterprise compliance". Integrated with Meta, Snapchat, TikTok, YouTube.
- Pricing view: Fortune-500 budgets, >$1M annual influencer spend. Multi-year contracts. 
- TiKiT response: Lean on engineering-grade approvals, risk layers, PDPL-ready audit logs. Offer Arabic/English UI, faster deployment (<8 weeks vs 6+ months), local support.

### GRIN (Commerce Automation)
- Sources: [Grin pricing](https://grin.co/pricing/), G2/Capterra reviews.
- Strength: Shopify-first workflow (product seeding, discount code automation, sales attribution). Built for lean DTC teams without agencies.
- Pricing: entry around $2.5K/mo; enterprise tiers higher.
- TiKiT action: ensure ecommerce hooks (Shopify, Woo, custom) via Supabase functions + automation templates, but keep agency-first flows (multi-client, approvals, financial governance).

### Aspire (Meta Preferred Partner)
- Sources: [Aspire preferred partner page](https://www.aspire.io/preferred-influencer-marketing-partner), Shopify app listing, pricing blogs.
- Differentiators: direct allowlisting (Meta), paid social boosting, built-in marketplace.
- Pricing: published analyses cite ~$2K–$4K/mo plus services; charges per seat/campaign.
- TiKiT action: highlight custom OS + ownership vs marketplace lock-in. Build connectors for allowlisting later but sell on operations discipline now.

### Dash Social (Predictive Ops)
- Source: [Dash Social CreatorIQ alternatives article](https://www.dashsocial.com/blog/creatoriq-alternatives).
- Strengths: AI predictions, EMV tracking, intuitive UI.
- TiKiT action: ensure analytics dashboards show predictive insights (use Supabase materialized views + Gemini for recommendations). Emphasize reliability + Gulf-sector specialization.

### HypeAuditor
- Source: [HypeAuditor site/pricing](https://hypeauditor.com/).
- Offering: global creator DB, fraud detection, automated outreach/reporting, competitor insights.
- Pricing: tiered SaaS; APIs for deeper data.
- TiKiT action: integrate fraud/risk scoring (Gemini + Supabase heuristics) and highlight "risk aware" story in PRD.

### Modash
- Sources: [Modash pricing](https://www.modash.io/pricing), [InfluencerForum](https://theinfluencerforum.com/modash-pricing-whats-included-in-the-plans/).
- Offering: 200M+ creators, IG/TikTok/YT >1K followers, Shopify features, API products (Discovery API $16.2K/yr).
- Pricing: $120/mo entry, scaling to $399+ for performance plans, APIs 5-figure annual.
- TiKiT action: Provide curated GCC creator datasets (Gemini + Supabase) + possibility of future API for agencies.

### Later Influence (Mavrck)
- Sources: [Later pricing](https://later.com/influencer-marketing-platform/pricing/), G2 reviews.
- Offering: Manage influencers/loyalists at scale, automated communications, measurement services.
- Pricing: custom, includes managed service components.
- TiKiT action: highlight that agencies keep control + IP; TiKiT automates internal ops without outsourcing to Later.

### Captiv8
- Sources: [Captiv8 site](https://captiv8.io/), G2 reviews.
- Offering: discovery, direct messaging, automated reporting, payments, Shopify integration.
- Pricing: enterprise (est. $3K+/mo). Known for full-service brand deals.
- TiKiT action: build Finance spine (Supabase) with auto invoices, bank/PayPal tracking, Dirham/SAR-ready.

### Pixis / Archive
- Source: [Pixis blog](https://pixis.ai/blog/best-ugc-influencer-marketing-platforms-2026/).
- Offering: Archive Radar (100% IG, 98% TikTok tag capture), AI tagging, ROI proof.
- Pricing: enterprise SaaS. Archive claims 40% more UGC captured.
- TiKiT action: add KPI scheduler + automatic proof collection (Supabase storage + cron). Use as future add-on.

### InfluenceFlow
- Source: [InfluenceFlow 2026 guide](https://influenceflow.io/resources/instagram-analytics-tools-and-competitor-analysis-the-complete-2026-guide-2/).
- Offering: Instagram analytics, competitor benchmarking, growth guides.
- TiKiT action: integrate benchmarking module comparing TiKiT campaigns vs industry metrics for client reports.
