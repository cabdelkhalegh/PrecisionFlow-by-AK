# TiKiT OS Competitor Watch (v0.1)

_Last updated: 2026-02-10_

This file tracks the most relevant influencer marketing / creator ops platforms TiKiT must monitor. Each entry captures positioning, key strengths, pricing signals (when public), and what TiKiT needs to counter/differentiate.

| Platform | Positioning | Key Strengths | Pricing / Target | Relevance to TiKiT |
|----------|-------------|----------------|------------------|--------------------|
| **CreatorIQ** | Enterprise influencer operating system used by Fortune 500 brands. | Deep compliance, enterprise integrations, multi-market governance, advanced reporting. | Custom/enterprise. | Competes on governance & scale; TiKiT must offer similar audit + approval depth for MENA agencies. |
| **GRIN** | E-commerce-first creator management suite with Shopify focus. | Product seeding automation, sales attribution, strong store integrations. | Starts ~$2.5K/mo (est); targets large DTC brands. | Less relevant for agency workflows but sets expectations for commerce integrations. |
| **Aspire** | High-touch influencer relationship platform with Meta partnerships. | Creator marketplace, allowlisting, paid social workflows. | Mid-to-high tier SaaS. | Threat for agencies wanting built-in talent marketplace; TiKiT differentiates via custom ops + AI governance. |
| **Dash Social** | All-in-one social + influencer ops with predictive analytics (Dash article). | Creator vetting, EMV tracking, performance prediction baked in. | Pricing not public; positioned for beauty/fashion/CPG. | Their "can do it all" messaging overlaps with TiKiT—need to emphasize engineering-grade workflows + ownership. |
| **HypeAuditor** | AI-powered analytics + outreach automation. | Large creator database, fraud detection, automated outreach, competitive insights. | Mix of free tools + paid tiers. | Good benchmark for data depth; TiKiT should integrate similar intelligence modules. |
| **Modash** | Mid-market platform focused on discovery + performance, strong Shopify integration. | AI discovery, audience filters, automated reporting. | Entry tier ~$199/mo. | Highlights demand for API-friendly data; TiKiT can position as the custom/owned alternative. |
| **InfluenceFlow** | Analytics + competitor benchmarking (2026 guide). | Instagram analytics, benchmarking, reporting templates. | SaaS tiers (not public). | Shows appetite for competitor tracking; TiKiT’s analytics module should include similar benchmarking. |
| **Pixis / Archive** | Capture & organize UGC automatically. | AI video recognition, auto-tagging, social listening. | Archive: SaaS with enterprise pricing; Pixis positions as AI marketing suite. | Opportunity to integrate UGC capture later; note their claim of 400% more content tracked. |
