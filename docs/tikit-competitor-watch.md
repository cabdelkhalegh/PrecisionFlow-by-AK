# TiKiT OS - Competitor Watchlist & Attack Plan

## 🚨 Primary Targets (The Overpriced Incumbents)

### 1. GRIN
- **Price:** Hidden (starts ~$500/mo, annual contracts only).
- **Core Pitch:** "Creator Management Platform."
- **Weakness:** Extremely expensive, no monthly options, heavy focus on "E-commerce" integration (Shopify) rather than general agency workflow.
- **TiKiT Attack:** "We are $49/mo. No annual contract. Use us for ANY client, not just Shopify brands."

### 2. Influencer Hero
- **Price:** $649/mo (Starter).
- **Core Pitch:** "All-in-one AI."
- **Weakness:** The AI is mostly just search/discovery. The "CRM" is clunky.
- **TiKiT Attack:** "Our AI actually *negotiates* and *writes briefs*. Theirs just finds emails."

### 3. Upfluence
- **Price:** $1,000+/mo.
- **Core Pitch:** Enterprise database.
- **Weakness:** Bloated. Slow. Requires sales demo to buy.
- **TiKiT Attack:** "Self-serve. Sign up today. Start running campaigns in 5 minutes."
