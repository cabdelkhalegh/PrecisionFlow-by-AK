## Executive Overview

### The Situation

**TikiT Agency** approached PrecisionFlow to build a custom campaign management platform (TiKiT OS) to solve their operational chaos:

**Current Pain Points**:
- Managing 20+ concurrent influencer campaigns across 10+ fragmented tools
- 60% of team time wasted on manual administration
- Campaign delays, approval bottlenecks, execution errors
- Cannot scale without proportional cost increase (need 2-3 more staff for 30 campaigns/month)
- Annual cost of inefficiency: **$463,000/year**
- Lost revenue opportunities: **$500,000/year** (capacity constraint)

**Our Solution**: Build TiKiT OS - A custom internal campaign execution platform

**Operational Impact**:
- 60% efficiency gain
- 3x campaign capacity (20 → 35 campaigns/month with same team)
- $245K annual cost savings
- $200K+ revenue increase potential
- Real-time visibility and automation
