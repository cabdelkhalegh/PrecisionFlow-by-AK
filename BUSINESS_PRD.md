## 5. Product Overview

### What is TiKiT OS?

TiKiT OS is an **enterprise-grade operating system for influencer marketing agencies** that treats the campaign as the single container for all execution activities. It provides:

✅ **Campaign Management**: Create, track, and manage campaigns from brief to closure  
✅ **AI-Powered Intelligence**: Automated brief structuring, creator matching, and learning  
✅ **Workflow Automation**: Multi-layer approval gates with complete governance  
✅ **Creator Management**: Database, shortlisting, contracting, and performance tracking  
✅ **Content Lifecycle**: Script → Draft → Final with approval gates  
✅ **Financial Tracking**: Budget, expenses, invoices, payments per campaign  
✅ **Real-time Visibility**: Live dashboards for teams and clients  
✅ **Learning Engine**: Capture and apply insights across campaigns  
✅ **Audit Trail**: Complete transparency of who did what and when

### Core Principle

> **"Campaign is the OS container. Everything is orchestrated through Campaign."**

Every entity in TiKiT OS exists within the context of a campaign:
- Briefs belong to campaigns
- Creators are assigned to campaigns
- Content tasks are part of campaigns
- Financial objects are linked to campaigns
- Approvals are for campaign artifacts
- Learnings are extracted from campaigns

This ensures:
- **Single source of truth** for all campaign data
- **No data silos** or fragmented information
- **Complete traceability** of all actions
- **Client transparency** through campaign-centric reporting

- **Monitoring**: Vercel Analytics, Sentry, LogRocket
- **CI/CD**: GitHub Actions

**Development**:
- **Language**: TypeScript 5.0+ (strict mode)
- **Monorepo**: Turborepo + pnpm workspaces
- **Testing**: Vitest (unit), Playwright (E2E)
- **Code Quality**: ESLint, Prettier, Husky
