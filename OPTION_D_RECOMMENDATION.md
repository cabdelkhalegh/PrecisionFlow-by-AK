### How Revenue-Share Works

**Baseline Assumptions**:
- TikiT Agency runs campaigns worth ~$50K average
- Current volume: 20 campaigns/month
- Post-TiKiT OS: 25-35 campaigns/month (scalability benefit)
- Revenue share: 6% of campaign value

**Example**:
```
Campaign Value: $50,000
PrecisionFlow receives: $3,000 (6%)
TikiT keeps: $47,000
Both benefit from campaign success
```

**Monthly Revenue Projection**:
- Month 7-9: 22 campaigns × $50K × 6% = $66,000/month
- Month 12: 25 campaigns × $50K × 6% = $75,000/month  
- Year 2: 28 campaigns × $50K × 6% = $84,000/month
- Year 3: 35 campaigns × $50K × 6% = $105,000/month

**By Year 3, we're earning $105K/month = $1.26M/year in recurring revenue.**
